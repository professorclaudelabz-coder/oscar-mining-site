The operation

A working mine, run like a ledger.

Gold has been won from these hills for generations — in quartz veins through the old basement rock, and in the river gravels below. Oscar Mining SO works both: a benched open pit on the vein system, with controlled recovery of the alluvials.

What sets us apart isn't the geology. It's the discipline: every tonne logged, every pour weighed and witnessed, every month reported to the people who backed us.

Responsibility

Reclaim your future.

It's on our logo because it's the plan. Before profit is taken, 2.5% of every pour — 461 oz so far this year — is set aside for the communities of Sanaag and the land we work.

Land, rehabilitated

Mined ground is re-shaped, capped and re-seeded behind the pit as we go — photographed and published in the monthly report.

Hills hired first

Jobs and paid training go first to the surrounding districts — labourer to plant operator, surveyor, geologist's assistant.

Fund what lasts

Schools, clinic supplies and boreholes, chosen with district elders and reported in the same ledger as the gold.
