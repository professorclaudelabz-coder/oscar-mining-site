Field notes

Two kinds of gold, one discipline.

In the rock: quartz veins through crystalline basement, worked as a benched open pit — drill, blast, haul, surveyed at every step.

In the gravels: alluvial gold shed from those veins into the dry riverbeds, recovered in controlled washing campaigns after the rains.

The discipline is the same for both: nothing moves without being counted, and everything counted lands in the monthly report.
